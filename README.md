# Pharmacovigilance Internship – Task 3

## Causality Assessment

### Objective

To evaluate the relationship between a suspected drug and an adverse reaction using standard causality assessment methods.

### Case Summary

- Patient: Raj
- Suspected drug: Amoxicillin
- Dose: 500 mg three times daily
- Indication: Throat infection

### Case Timeline

- 01 June 2021: Amoxicillin 500 mg three times daily was started for throat infection.
- 02 June 2021: Patient developed generalized itchy rashes.
- 03 June 2021: Dose was reduced to 250 mg three times daily.
- 04 June 2021: Rash disappeared.
- 05 June 2021: Dose was increased again to 500 mg three times daily because the intensity of the infection increased.
- 06 June 2021: Patient developed generalized itchy rashes again.

### Causality Assessment

The case demonstrates:

- Positive temporal relationship
- Positive dechallenge
- Positive rechallenge
- Apparent dose-response relationship

### Naranjo Assessment

The tutorial exercise resulted in a total Naranjo score of 9.

According to the scoring interpretation used in the tutorial:

- 9 or greater: Definite
- 5–8: Probable
- 1–4: Possible
- 0: Doubtful

Therefore, the tutorial exercise classifies the case as **Definite** based on a Naranjo score of 9.

### Learning Points

- Temporal relationship
- Dechallenge
- Rechallenge
- Dose-response relationship
- Causality assessment
- Naranjo ADR Probability Scale
- Assessment of drug-event relationships
